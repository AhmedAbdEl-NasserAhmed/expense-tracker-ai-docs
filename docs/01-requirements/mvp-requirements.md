# MVP Requirements

## Core Promise

The MVP must help the user understand their current financial position, record spending with low friction, and build reliable financial history.

## Must Have

- Email/password authentication.
- Google authentication.
- Email password reset.
- Account deletion.
- Initial financial profile onboarding.
- Multiple income streams.
- One-time income and ongoing income streams.
- Current savings.
- Fixed commitments and recurring expenses.
- One currency per user.
- Predefined and custom expense categories.
- Expense creation with amount, category, payment method, and transaction date.
- Historical expense dates.
- Expense editing and deletion.
- Multiple-expense entry.
- Recurring expense rules.
- Credit-card liability tracking at the expense level.
- Current-month spending capacity.
- Daily spending guidance.
- Overspending visibility without blocking the user.
- User-confirmed handling of savings changes.
- Missing-day tracking with explicit no-spending and unknown states.
- Current-month dashboard.
- Detailed expense history.
- Monthly overview/history.
- One daily expense-tracking reminder.
- Goals as a separate feature, without requiring goals during onboarding.
- Three-month observation period before interpreting saving behavior for personalized guidance.

## Explicitly Excluded

- AI.
- AI recommendations.
- AI purchase decision assistant.
- AI chatbot.
- Bank synchronization.
- Automatic transaction import.
- Full bank-account management.
- Receipt OCR.
- Voice expense recording.
- Investment tracking.
- Family accounts.
- Subscription detection.
- Advanced forecasting.
- Financial health score.
- Automatic treatment of unused capacity as savings.
- Multi-currency support.

## Open Questions for Later Design

- Exact calculation and treatment of partial-month recurring commitments.
- Exact monthly treatment of income/expenses when effective dates fall inside a month.
- Exact credit-card statement/payment model.
- Exact data-retention and deletion implementation.
- Exact notification delivery timing.
- Exact statistics and chart definitions.
- Exact goal data model and goal calculations.
