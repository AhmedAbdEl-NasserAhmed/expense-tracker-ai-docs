# Product Constraints

These constraints define the boundaries of the MVP.

## Functional Constraints
- Manual expense tracking only.
- Single user accounts.
- Financial goals are managed separately from onboarding.
- Recommendations require sufficient historical data.

## Technical Constraints
- Responsive web application.
- English language only for MVP.
- No offline support.
- No bank integrations.
- No receipt OCR.

## Scope Constraints
Features such as AI chat, subscription detection, family accounts, investment tracking, and bank synchronization are intentionally deferred until after the MVP.
