# Architectural & Product Decisions

## Decision #001

### Title

Financial goals are not collected during onboarding.

### Reason

Users should first understand their financial situation before defining future financial goals.

Financial goals will exist as a separate feature.

---

## Decision #002

### Title

Recommendations begin after one month.

### Reason

The application requires enough financial history before generating personalized recommendations.

---

## Decision #003

### Title

Expense categories are predefined with custom additions.

### Reason

Reduce onboarding friction while allowing flexibility for unique spending categories.

---

## Decision #004

### Title

The onboarding process builds a financial profile.

### Reason

The application must understand the user's financial situation before providing guidance.

The onboarding collects:

- Income
- Income sources
- Savings
- Fixed commitments
- Recurring expense categories
- Marital status
